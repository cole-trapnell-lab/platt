# Source of the empirical-FDR schematic images

`efdr_within_state_test.png` (panels A–D), `efdr_thin_arm_problem.png` (panel E) and
`efdr_null_protocol.png` (panels F–H) are crops of Supplementary Figure S9 of the Hooke/Platt
manuscript, rendered at 400 dpi from
`hooke_revision/Figures/supp09_efdr_protocol/supp_figure_9.ai` (the individual panel PDFs are
drawn by `plot_9AH.R` in that folder; the titles and letters are added in the .ai).

`efdr_mechanism.png` and `efdr_calibration.png` are the real-data diagnostics from the same
figure (panels I and K); `degs_over_graph.png` is Supplementary Figure S8A.
